About EatAware

We Decode What India Eats

EatAware is India's first Ingredient Intelligence Platform — built so that every Indian consumer can understand exactly what is inside their food and what it does to their body, today and over time.

Why We Built This

India has over 1.4 billion consumers who pick up packaged food every day — biscuits, noodles, chips, health drinks — and read nothing on the label beyond the brand name and price.

Yet the ingredients list on the back of every product tells the complete story — which oils were used, which preservatives were added, which dyes colour the product, and what long-term effects daily consumption creates in the body.

EatAware exists to translate that ingredient list from chemical jargon into plain, honest English backed by evidence — and to give every product a single honest grade that tells you where it stands.

No Brand Influence
We accept no payment from food brands. Our grades are based purely on ingredients and research — never on who pays us.
Evidence-Based
Every ingredient effect we describe is sourced from published clinical research, FSSAI standards, and WHO guidelines — not opinion.
Plain Language
We translate "Tertiary Butylhydroquinone" into "Synthetic preservative — caution with daily use" so a 50-year-old parent understands it as clearly as a nutritionist.
Our Methodology

How We Grade Every Product A to F

Our grading system is an EatAware editorial model built on FSSAI label data, WHO nutritional guidance, and peer-reviewed public-health evidence — calibrated for Indian dietary patterns.

1
We read the back of the product label
Every rating starts with the ingredients list on the back of the actual physical product — not marketing claims on the front. Ingredients are listed in descending order of weight.
2
We apply evidence-based scoring
Each ingredient is assessed against FSSAI permit data, WHO guidelines, IARC classifications, and peer-reviewed nutrition research. Penalties for harmful additives; bonuses for whole foods and probiotics.
3
We assign a single A–F grade
A final score out of 100 maps to a grade. Hard caps apply — e.g. any product with confirmed trans fats is automatically capped at F regardless of other ingredients.
